How to Use

Practice nursing dosage calculations at your own pace. No account is needed.

Start Session
  1. 1Choose Practice or Quiz.
  2. 2Choose your difficulty and what you want to practice.
  3. 3Choose 5, 10, 15, 20, or 25 questions.
  4. 4Enter your answer, submit it, and review the worked solution.
Start Practice

Practice is best while you are learning. While working a question, you can tap See Formula to view the formula before answering.

Quiz is best when you want to test yourself without seeing the formula before you answer.

Both modes tell you whether your answer is correct and show the worked solution after you submit it.

Build Your Session

Difficulty: Easy, Standard, or Challenge. Difficulty changes how convenient the numbers are, not the nursing logic.

Choose what to practice:

  • Medication Dosing — medication amounts, reconstitution, and electrolyte volumes
  • Pediatric Dosing — weight-based dosing, safe-dose ranges, and divided doses
  • Infusion Rates — pump rates, gravity rates, mL/hr, gtt/min, and other infusion-rate calculations
  • IV Time & Volume — infusion duration and completion time

Choose a whole family for variety, choose one subtype to drill a specific skill, or use Random Mix — All Families.

Answering Questions

For most questions, enter the number and unit.

4.7 mL
125 mL/hr

The unit matters. A correct number with the wrong unit is not a complete dosage-calculation answer.

For IV completion-time questions, enter 24-hour time:

16:30

For pediatric safe-dose questions, you may first choose Safe, Too Low, or Too High. If the dose is safe, you may then calculate the amount to administer.

After You Answer

The program shows whether your answer is Correct or Incorrect, followed by the correct answer, formula, and worked calculation.

Choose Next Question to continue. At the end, you will see your score and percentage.

Formula Review

Choose Formula Review from the home page when you want to study the dosage calculation formulas without starting a practice session.

Instructor Quizzes

Choose Instructor Quizzes to see quizzes that have been prepared and published by your instructor.

Select the assigned quiz and work through it one question at a time. Your score and percentage are shown when the quiz is complete.

If no quizzes are currently published, the page will tell you.

Helpful Reminders
  • Read the entire question before calculating.
  • Pay close attention to units.
  • Convert units when necessary before solving.
  • Follow any rounding directions in the question.
  • Enter the requested unit with your answer.
  • If you miss a question, review the worked solution before moving on.

Practice Only

This program is for education and dosage-calculation practice only. Do not use it to determine or verify medication doses for actual patient care. Always follow your instructor's directions and your clinical facility's medication-administration policies.
